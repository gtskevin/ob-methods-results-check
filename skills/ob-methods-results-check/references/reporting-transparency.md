# Results Interpretation and Reporting

Build the hypothesis evidence table in [report-template.md](report-template.md). For each focal claim inspect:

1. Target effect and actual test: difference, association, interaction, indirect effect or change. A coefficient with the opposite sign does not support a directional hypothesis even when significant.
2. Coefficient scale, reference group/value, units, analysis-specific N, SE, CI, exact p (appropriate reporting precision), df and effect-size definition. Keep statistical significance separate from magnitude and practical relevance; wide CIs may leave important uncertainty.
3. Nonsignificance is not proof of no effect. An absence/equivalence claim needs appropriate evidence, such as a justified equivalence margin/test or Bayesian comparison with stated assumptions; do not demand these for every nonsignificant result.
4. Direct contrasts for claims that effects differ; separate significant/nonsignificant results or separate CI decisions are not sufficient. Consult the relevant method reference for the proper contrast.
5. Confirmatory versus exploratory analyses, preregistration deviations, multiple outcomes/comparisons and analysis selection. Assess multiplicity against the claim and inferential family rather than automatically applying one correction everywhere.
6. Robustness against a named threat: show justified alternative specifications and material changes, not only those yielding significance. A methodologically inadequate design is not repaired merely by more models or softened wording.
7. Consistency across abstract, hypotheses, Methods, Results, tables, figures, appendices and software output; outcome labels must distinguish intentions/perceptions from behavior. Causal/generalization claims must stay within design and sampling support.
8. Sample flow/exclusions/missingness, scale anchors/scoring/adaptations, reliability/measurement information relevant to the model, table notes and software/estimation details. Do not require CR/AVE or every conventional index regardless of purpose.

Evidence status and severity are independent. Missing output means unresolved estimation adequacy, not a confirmed analysis error; manuscript-only checks are not reproduction. Explain the smallest repair and necessary artifact. For target-journal rules, read [journal-standards.md](journal-standards.md).
