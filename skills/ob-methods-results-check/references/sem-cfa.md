# CFA and SEM Audit

1. Reconcile constructs, items, indicators/parcels, fixed/free parameters, identification and degrees of freedom with syntax. Use the helper only for conventional correlated-factor CFA; a df discrepancy is a reason to inspect syntax, not automatic proof of error.
2. Match estimator to indicator type, distribution and design; inspect missing-data treatment, clustering and standardization. Ordinal indicators require a justified treatment, not an automatic universal estimator choice.
3. Inspect convergence, improper solutions (e.g., negative residual variances), loadings, factor correlations and local residuals as well as global fit. Conventional CFI/RMSEA/SRMR cutoffs are context-dependent evidence, not mechanical proof of validity.
4. Check the theoretical and measurement rationale for item deletion, parceling, correlated residuals and post-hoc modifications; distinguish exploratory respecification from confirmation. Parcels must not conceal an unsupported measurement structure.
5. Identify meaningful alternative models and valid comparison procedures for the estimator/model relationship. Report fit statistics, df and relevant uncertainty; do not compare models mechanically with an inapplicable chi-square difference test.
6. A saturated (zero-df) path model's perfect fit cannot validate the theory. Good fit neither identifies causal direction nor proves the model is uniquely correct.
7. Distinguish latent/observed and standardized/unstandardized coefficients; report structural paths with uncertainty. Check measurement coverage of substantive multi-item controls and adapted constructs without automatically requiring every measure in every CFA.

For reliability/discriminant validity/invariance, read [measurement-quality.md](measurement-quality.md). For choice of path analysis versus latent SEM, read [regression-path.md](regression-path.md). Sources: [organizational SEM review](https://doi.org/10.1146/annurev-orgpsych-041621-031401); [JARS SEM reporting table](https://apastyle.apa.org/jars/quant-table-7.pdf).
