# Experiment Audit

## Design and selection

- Record random assignment unit, recruitment, stopping/sample-size rationale, allocation, cell sizes, preregistration and deviations. Distinguish independent, paired, factorial, repeated-measures and cluster-randomized designs.
- Examine materials for construct validity and plausible confounds, including demand effects and manipulation-check placement. Distinguish behavior, intention, perception and manipulation-check outcomes.
- Inspect exclusions and covariate timing. Removing failed manipulation checks can select on a post-treatment variable and undermine the randomized comparison; preregistration alone does not remove this problem. Request full randomized-sample analysis where feasible, exclusion counts by condition, rules and justified sensitivity analyses. Do not infer bias direction or a reversed conclusion without evidence.

## Model and contrast

- Match t/ANOVA/ANCOVA/regression/mixed models to allocation and dependence. Check variance assumptions (e.g., Welch where appropriate), covariate role, and within-person/stimulus dependence. For repeated-measures ANOVA inspect sphericity/corrections when applicable; for mixed models inspect justified and estimable random effects.
- Map each factorial hypothesis to its interaction or planned contrast. Separate omnibus tests, planned comparisons and exploratory follow-ups; consider multiplicity in light of the inferential family. Separate significance within conditions is not a test of treatment-effect difference.
- Verify cell/marginal means using the correct weighting or adjusted-mean definition, df, direction, coefficient units, effect-size definition (d, eta-squared, partial eta-squared) and CIs. Do not demand that adjusted means equal raw weighted means.
- Randomization of X or W does not automatically identify the entire mediation chain. Apply [mediation-moderation.md](mediation-moderation.md) before causal mechanism claims.

Reporting anchor: [JARS experimental designs](https://apastyle.apa.org/jars/quant-table-2.pdf). Use [journal-standards.md](journal-standards.md) for journal-specific requirements.
