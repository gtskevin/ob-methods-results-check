# Journal Requirements versus Methodological Judgment

Load only for target-journal submission checks or when a claimed norm affects an audit judgment.

Classify the basis of advice: (1) explicit target-journal requirement, (2) statistical/design principle, (3) conditional methodological recommendation, or (4) published example/contested convention. Cite the relevant source and applicability; a published practice is not a universal rule. Verify the current official page before asserting a current journal requirement. If unavailable, mark the dated information below as unverified-current.

## JAP snapshot: checked 2026-10-10

[JAP submission page](https://www.apa.org/pubs/journals/apl/submit) requires its [Methods Checklist](https://www.apa.org/pubs/journals/features/apl-manuscript-checklist.pdf) and applicable [JARS standards](https://apastyle.apa.org/jars/quantitative). Use these as a reporting overlay, not an automatic correctness verdict.

The checklist asks for rationale for mono-level path analysis over latent-variable SEM and, if possible, supplemental SEM. Flag missing rationale for JAP; do not declare path analysis invalid or force latent SEM regardless of measurement, estimand and data.

The checklist also requests observed power when possible. Report this journal-specific item separately from methodological validity: observed-effect post-hoc power is not independent evidence of replicability or absence of an effect. Prefer informative design-stage power planning, effect CIs and correctly interpreted sensitivity analyses. A detectable-effect threshold is not a bound on the true effect. See [Hoenig & Heisey (2001)](https://doi.org/10.1198/000313001300339897).

Do not transfer JAP-specific requirements to AMJ, OBHDP or other journals without checking their guidance. Do not impose a universal alpha/fit cutoff, mandatory SEM, mandatory preregistration or a fixed battery of robustness tests merely because some papers use them.
